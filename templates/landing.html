Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about VoiceDesk AI implementation, accents, and accuracy.

How does VoiceDesk prevent the agent from speaking with an English accent?

VoiceDesk converts all spoken responses into native Urdu Nastaliq script before passing them to the neural synthesis engine. When TTS engines read Urdu script rather than Latin/Roman characters, retroflex consonants and vowels are pronounced natively with authentic Pakistani cadence.

What happens if a customer asks for a service or discount we do not offer?

VoiceDesk enforces a strict zero-hallucination guardrail. If an inquiry is not listed in your verified business catalog (for example: asking a dental clinic for pizza or unlisted discounts), the agent is prohibited from guessing and immediately gives your configured fallback response.

Can we clone our own founder's or front desk receptionist's voice?

Yes. Simply record a 15–30 second audio clip directly in our studio. Groq Whisper automatically transcribes the audio to provide 100% phonetic forced alignment, preserving your natural vocal timbre without robotic distortion.

How does physical thermal paper receipt generation work?

When an order or booking is confirmed during a call, VoiceDesk automatically computes 5% GST, calculates the grand total in PKR, and renders an authentic thermal receipt image with a barcode and rubber stamp proof ready to print or send.

Can callers interrupt the agent while it is speaking?

Yes. VoiceDesk features full-duplex telephony with acoustic barge-in. The moment the caller begins speaking, the agent halts its speech buffer immediately and listens attentively, just like a real person on the phone.

How do I connect my existing Pakistani business phone number or WhatsApp?

VoiceDesk connects with standard SIP trunks, Twilio/Telnyx Pakistani DIDs, and the WhatsApp Business Cloud API. You can forward your existing business number directly to your VoiceDesk agent with zero hardware changes.
